# Power BI connection

## Summary

IOMETE documents three ways to connect Power BI: Arrow Flight with the IOMETE Arrow Flight ODBC
connector (recommended), Thrift with Power BI's built-in Spark connector, and Thrift ODBC with the
Simba Spark ODBC driver.

## Details

- Recommended method: Arrow Flight.
